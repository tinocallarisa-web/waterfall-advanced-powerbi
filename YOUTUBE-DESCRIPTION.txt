Waterfall Advanced 1.2 for Power BI — P&L bridges with totals, subtotals and drivers


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DESCRIPTION (paste into the YouTube description field)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Power BI's built-in waterfall handles category movement. It does not handle a
bridge: an opening anchor, the drivers that explain the movement, the intermediate
checkpoints a finance reader expects, and a closing total. Without those, a P&L
walk is a row of floating bars and the reader has to reconstruct the story
themselves.

Waterfall Advanced adds the one thing that changes that — semantic control over
what each bar means. Mark a row as a total, a subtotal or a driver, and the chart
draws the bridge your finance team already has in their heads.

NEW IN 1.2
• Five style templates — Classic, Modern, Soft, One colour (with a base colour
  you pick) and Dark background
• Model format: the measure's own format string, dynamic format strings included
• Value axis (gridlines, font, colour, title), category axis title and a legend
  you can move to the top or bottom
• On-object formatting: click bars, labels, axis, legend, cards or table
• Value labels and the target line drawn above the bars, with a halo
• Pro preview: try the data table, IBCS and label density on your own data while
  you edit
• 12 languages, a start screen and setup tips

WHAT IT DOES
• Totals, subtotals and drivers as distinct bar types, driven by a column in your
  model — not guessed from position
• Intermediate checkpoints drawn from zero, so a reader sees where the margin
  stood before the next block of costs
• Horizontal or vertical, because line-item names read better down the side
• Hierarchical drilldown
• A target line on the total bars, with its own label and figure
• Drivers beyond two standard deviations from the mean marked automatically
• Summary cards: initial, final, variance %, biggest gain, biggest loss
• Conditional formatting on bar colour through the native fx dialog, or a hex
  colour per row calculated in your model

READABLE ON PURPOSE
A colour-blind safe palette and hatched decreases, both in the free version. With
the hatch on, a rise and a fall differ by pattern as well as colour, so the chart
still reads printed in black and white.

IN THE REPORT
Cross-filtering with multi-select, cross-highlighting, bookmarks, report page
tooltips, keyboard navigation and high contrast. Format pane and texts in 12
languages.

PRIVACY
No network calls of any kind: no telemetry, no analytics, no endpoints. All
calculation and rendering happens inside Power BI.

FREE AND PRO
The free version is the whole bridge, with up to twelve individual drivers. Above
twelve the smallest drivers are grouped into an "Others" bar: the bridge still
closes on the same figure, the detail is coarser.

Pro adds every driver individually, the data table, IBCS mode and the label
density controls. Without a licence you can try them on your own data while you
edit, under a "Pro preview" watermark; readers see the free chart.
