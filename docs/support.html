Waterfall Advanced draws a financial bridge: an opening total, the drivers that move it, checkpoints such as Net Revenue, Gross Profit or EBITDA, and a closing total. You tell it which rows are totals, subtotals and drivers with one field, and the chart draws the walk from the opening figure to the closing one.

The chart is complete without a licence, with up to 12 individual drivers. A Pro plan shows every driver individually and adds a data table, IBCS mode and label density settings. See Free and Pro.

Quick start

  1. Add Waterfall Advanced from AppSource (Visualizations pane → Get more visuals).
  2. Drag the P&L line, driver or step into Category.
  3. Drag the amount into Value. Positive values rise, negative values fall.
  4. Optional: drag a column holding total, subtotal or delta into Bar type and set it to aggregate as First, not Sum.
  5. Optional: drag a numeric field into Order to fix the sequence of the bridge.
  6. In the Format pane, Style template changes the look in one click.

With no fields, the visual shows a start screen with these steps and links to this guide, the product page and the video; the links open only when you click them. While you edit, a tips bar at the bottom offers the templates and “Try Pro” buttons; hide it with ✕ (it comes back from Format → Style template → Show setup tips). It never shows in reading view.

Bar types: totals, subtotals and drivers

Value in Bar typeWhat it drawsUse it for
totalA bar from zero. The first one sets the opening figure; later ones show the running total.Opening and closing figures
subtotalA bar from zero to the running total, without changing itNet Revenue, Gross Profit, EBITDA
delta (or anything else)A floating bar from the running totalEvery driver: price, volume, payroll, freight

Without a Bar type field, the first and last rows are totals and everything in between is a driver — a simple opening-to-closing walk. With the field, mark the first and last rows total yourself, and the checkpoints subtotal. The text is read without regard to case or surrounding spaces.

The Bar type must arrive as text. Power BI tries to add up a text field in a measure well, so set it to First (or write a measure that returns the text).

Pro preview

Without a licence you can still try the data table, IBCS mode and the label density settings on your own data:

  • While you edit, a Pro setting you turn on is drawn working, under a “Pro preview” watermark that names it, and Power BI shows its licence notice and then an Upgrade bar.
  • In reading view, readers see the free chart: no watermark, no data table, no IBCS, no label density.
  • Where licences cannot be read (Publish to Web, embedding, export), the free result is drawn, with no watermark.
  • The grouping of drivers beyond 12 is not previewed: without a licence they are grouped in every mode.

The setting you turned on is kept. Once the licence is active, the feature works everywhere and the watermark goes.

On-object formatting

With Format on, click the bars, value labels, category labels, value axis, legend, summary cards or data table to see their options next to them, with a link to the card in the Format pane. In format mode a click chooses what to format and does not filter. As with native visuals, the first click selects the visual and the second the part.

Use cases

1. P&L walk with checkpoints

Revenue → discounts → subtotal Net Revenue → cost of sales → subtotal Gross Profit → operating costs → total EBITDA, with an Order column. Readers see where the margin stood before each block of costs.

2. Actual against budget

Put the budget in Target: the dashed line on the closing total shows whether the result reached it, and the tooltip gives the gap.

3. Revenue bridge: price, volume, mix

Last year, the effects of price, volume and mix, this year. Turn on Sort by impact to order the drivers by size.

Data table at the bottom with the running total and % of opening, and IBCS mode for a monochrome page that reads in print.

Troubleshooting

Every bar floats; there are no totals or subtotals

The Bar type field is aggregating as Sum or Count. In the field well, set it to First. Check that the text is exactly total, subtotal or delta.

The bars are in the wrong order

Add a numeric column to Order. Without it the bars follow the category sort, which is rarely the order of a P&L.

The closing bar is not what I expected
  • With a Bar type field, the first row must be total: it sets the opening figure.
  • Costs must be negative values to fall.
  • A subtotal shows the running total; it does not add its own value.
There is an “Others” bar I did not ask for

Either there are more than 12 drivers and no Pro licence, or Max drivers shown in the Chart card is set to a number. The total is correct in both cases.

Every bar has the same colour, and changing Decrease or Total colour does nothing

A fixed Increase colour saved by an earlier version was stored bar by bar and paints every bar. Pick any template in Format → Style template (Classic keeps the original look): this clears the old value.

I see “Pro preview” on the chart

You turned on the data table, IBCS mode or a label density setting without a licence. It is shown only to you while editing; readers see the free chart. Turn the setting off, or get the Pro plan to remove the watermark.

The summary cards or the legend are missing

In small tiles they hide themselves so the chart keeps its space. Make the visual larger. For the cards, check that both Chart → Summary cards and Summary Cards → Show summary cards are on.

The target line only appears on some bars

By design it is drawn on the total bars, where a goal is compared. Check also that Chart → Show target is on.

The tips bar covers my chart

It only appears while you edit. Close it with ✕ or turn off Format → Style template → Show setup tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? What does Pro add?

The bridge is complete without a licence, with up to 12 individual drivers, subtotals, target line, summary cards, templates, axes, legend and languages. Pro shows every driver individually and adds the data table, IBCS mode and the label density settings.

How do I buy Pro?

From the offer on Microsoft AppSource, plan pro, or from the Upgrade bar Power BI shows when you use a Pro feature. Prices and any trial are shown on the AppSource listing.

I bought Pro and still see the watermark or the “Others” bar

The licence is assigned in the Microsoft 365 admin center to the user who opens the report. Once assigned, close and reopen Power BI Desktop or reload the report in the service so Power BI reads the licence again. If it persists, write to support@tcviz.com.

Do readers of my report need a Pro licence?

The licence is checked for the person viewing the report. Viewers without a licence see the free chart, with no watermark — the report still works for them, without the Pro features.

What is “Pro preview”?

Without a licence, while you edit, the data table, IBCS mode and label density settings you turn on are drawn working under a watermark so you can try them on your data. In reading view, and where licences cannot be read (Publish to Web, embedding, export), the free result is drawn with no watermark. See Pro preview.

What happens with more than 12 drivers on the free version?

The 12 largest drivers are shown and the rest are summed into one “Others” bar, so the bridge still closes on the same figure. A note on the chart says how many were grouped. Totals and subtotals are never grouped. This grouping is not previewed: Pro shows every driver.

Can I group small drivers on purpose?

Yes: Chart → Max drivers shown. The rest go into the “Others” bar, whose name you set in “Others” bar label. Combine it with Sort by impact to keep the largest drivers in view.

How does Bar type work, and why “First”?

Each row says total, subtotal or delta; anything else is a driver. It is text, so in the field well it must be aggregated as First — Sum or Count return nothing usable and every bar becomes a driver. Without the field, the first and last rows are totals.

How do I fix the order of the bars?

Add a numeric column with the sequence (1, 2, 3…) to Order. Sort by impact, when on, reorders only the drivers between the first and last rows.

What is the difference between a total and a subtotal?

Both stand on zero. The first total sets the opening figure and later totals show where the walk has arrived; a subtotal is a checkpoint, such as Gross Profit, that shows the running total without changing it. Chart → Show subtotals off draws subtotals faded.

How does the target line work?

Put a budget or goal measure in Target. A dashed line is drawn on the total bars, with a label (Chart → Target label) and, optionally, its figure. The tooltip shows the target and the gap. If a total's value label would sit on the line, it moves inside the bar.

Can the numbers use the format of my measure?

Yes: Number format → Model format. Values, labels, tooltips and the axis then use the measure's format string, dynamic format strings included, in the report's language. Custom (currency symbol, scale, decimals, thousands separator) remains the default.

Does Model format support dynamic format strings?

Yes. If a DAX dynamic format string changes the format per row (for example a currency per country), each bar uses its own.

What do the style templates change, and how do I set the base colour?

A template sets the bar, label, axis and legend colours (Dark background also the cards and table). Pick One colour and set Base colour in the same card: totals take it, increases a lighter shade, decreases a darker one. Anything you change afterwards stays yours.

My report has a dark background. Which template should I use?

Dark background: brighter bars, light labels and halos, and dark summary cards and data table so they do not show as white boxes.

Can I colour bars with conditional formatting (fx)?

Yes: Colors → Increase color → fx, with a rule or a field. For full control, the Colour (DAX) well takes a hex colour per row and overrides both the fixed colours and the fx rule.

Every bar took my Increase colour. Why, and how do I fix it?

Before 1.2 a fixed Increase colour was stored bar by bar, through the same route the fx rule uses, so it covered totals and decreases too. In 1.2 fixed colours are stored for the whole visual. To clean an older report, pick a template (Classic keeps the original look): choosing a template clears the old per-bar value.

How do I format the axes?

The Value axis card shows or hides the axis and sets gridlines, font size, colour and a title (empty uses the measure name). The Category axis card adds a title (empty uses the field name). Category label colour and size are in Labels.

Where is the legend setting?

In the Colors card, group Legend: show or hide, top or bottom, font, size and colour. In very short tiles it hides itself.

Can I format by clicking on the chart?

Yes, with on-object formatting: bars, value labels, category labels, value axis, legend, summary cards and data table. See On-object formatting.

What do the summary cards show?

The initial and final figures, the variance %, the biggest gain and the biggest loss. They hide automatically in small tiles.

What is the red “!” on some bars?

The anomaly marker: a driver whose value is more than two standard deviations from the mean of the drivers.

Can I tilt or shorten long category labels?

With Pro: angle −30°, −45° or −90° (vertical orientation), truncation at a number of characters (the full name stays in the tooltip) and hiding labels that do not fit. Free alternative: switch to horizontal or lower the category font size.

What is IBCS mode? Pro

A monochrome notation: increases, decreases and totals in shades of grey, so the reader focuses on the deviation. It takes precedence over the template and palette colours.

What does the data table show? Pro

Each step with its amount and, optionally, the running total and the share of the opening figure, to the right of or below the chart. Width or height, font size, background and separator are in the Data Table card.

Do bookmarks and cross-filtering work?

Yes. Click a bar to filter (Ctrl adds), bookmarks restore the selection, and highlights from other visuals dim the bars outside them.

Can I use it with the keyboard?

Yes: Tab to the bars, Enter or Space to select. See accessibility.

Does it work with a date or product hierarchy?

Yes. Put the hierarchy in Category and use the drill-down buttons of the visual header.

Does the visual send my data anywhere?

No. It makes no network calls, has no telemetry, sets no cookies and stores nothing outside the report. The links on the start screen open only when you click them, through Power BI. See the privacy policy.
