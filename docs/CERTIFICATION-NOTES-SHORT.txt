WHAT CHANGED IN 1.1.3.0
A licensing fix only: no feature, well or rendering changed since the certified 1.1.2.0.

Power BI shows one notification at a time and the last call replaces the previous. The render
called notifyLicenseRequired and then notifyFeatureBlocked in the same update, so the notice
carrying the Upgrade button was replaced by the transient banner, and once the banner faded
there was no purchase path left. The sequence is now: clear, notifyFeatureBlocked naming the
setting, and 10.5 s later - banner gone - notifyLicenseRequired(0). The timer is cancelled on
destroy(), since Power BI recreates the visual on every page change.

NO LICENSING UI OF ITS OWN
No purchase UI and no watermark, per the guidance that a visual "shouldn't display its own
licensing UX". The only thing on the canvas is a neutral note saying the smaller drivers are
grouped and into what - a fact, not an offer, and needed or the totals would not match the
report. The free tier draws a complete, balanced bridge: it groups rather than hides, so
there is no partial Pro output to mark.

PRIVACY: no network calls, no telemetry, no storage. Only the dataView. privileges [].

FREE VS PRO
Free: the full bridge, 12 individual drivers (the rest grouped into one labelled step),
subtotals, totals, targets, tooltips, cross-filtering, keyboard nav, high contrast.
Pro: every driver individually, label rotation and truncation, hiding labels that do not fit,
IBCS mode, the data table.
