12 largest are drawn individually and the rest grouped into one labelled step, with a

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.1.3.0
A licensing fix only: no feature or rendering changed since the certified 1.1.2.0. The
render raised notifyLicenseRequired then notifyFeatureBlocked in the same update, and
Power BI shows one at a time, so the notice carrying the Upgrade button was replaced by
the transient banner and no purchase path was left. Now: clear, the banner, and the
Upgrade bar 10.5 s later, cancelled on destroy(). See CHANGELOG.md.

FREE VS PRO
Free: the full bridge - 12 largest drivers individually, the rest grouped into one
labelled step, subtotals, totals, targets, tooltips, cross-filtering, keyboard, high
contrast. It groups rather than hides: nothing is left out.

not fit, IBCS mode, data table.
