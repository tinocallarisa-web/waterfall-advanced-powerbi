Key benefits

  • It reconciles. Subtotals stand on zero, so the closing bar is the sum of what came before it.
  • It survives print. Hatched decreases read in black and white, in the pack that gets photocopied.
  • It stays inside Power BI. No telemetry, no endpoints, no CDN — nothing leaves the tenant.
  • The free tier is correct, not partial. Grouping beats cropping: a cropped bridge is a wrong bridge.

Tips

  • Set Bar type to aggregate as First. As Sum it returns blank and every bar becomes a driver.
  • Set the Order column to "Do not summarize", or the walk sorts alphabetically.
  • Go horizontal when line-item names are long — they read better down the side than rotated.
  • Turn the hatch on even with colour: it is the only cue that survives a greyscale printer.
